# Product Release Outline

Status: Artistic-first scope baseline. The standalone Artistic QR Studio is the primary major deliverable; broader infrastructure is deferred until it passes release.

## MVP — v1.0: Flagship Artistic QR Studio

- Premium standalone URL/text-to-artistic-QR workflow with no full account dependency; includes anonymous free preview and narrow guest one-time checkout/project access.
- Six or more meaningfully distinct, launch-quality art directions.
- Curated, prompt-guided, and validated reference-image workflows.
- Four-candidate exploration board with meaningful diversity.
- Variation, artistic strength, composition/focal area, palette, QR prominence, and supported seed controls.
- Protected QR functional mask plus closed-loop multi-decoder/perturbation validation and targeted repair.
- Honest robustness evidence, digital/print preview, minimum-size guidance, and deterministic safe fallback.
- Publication-ready high-resolution PNG and SVG for deterministic/vector designs.
- Local/session project continuity, provider/payment failure recovery, content/IP/privacy controls, accessibility, telemetry, and independent release proof.
- Approved monetization: $0 four-candidate preview, $12 project unlock, and contextual $5 Extra Exploration; fair successful-round accounting and all standard sizes included.

Explicitly excluded: full accounts, subscriptions, generic credits, durable cross-project entitlements, dynamic campaigns, redirects, analytics, custom domains, bulk generation, teams, and APIs.

## v1.1 — Artistic Pro

- Saved local projects and richer history/compare workflows.
- Reusable brand kits, palettes, logos, and approved creative presets.
- More art directions and stronger controlled variation.
- Improved print production, crop/placement previews, export dimensions, and applicable vector workflows.
- Stronger provenance and reproducibility.
- Quality improvements driven by real “proud to publish” and scan evidence.

## v1.2 — Creative Workflow and Monetization

- Optional accounts and cloud project sync.
- Transparent generation allowances, paid packs, or another validated monetization model.
- Share/review links and project library.
- Selected deterministic batch/template workflows.
- Customer support and purchase recovery appropriate to the adopted model.

## v2.0 — Dynamic QR Infrastructure

- One-time durable campaign purchase and entitlement.
- Editable redirect routes and lifecycle controls.
- Basic privacy-conscious scan analytics.
- Continuity, abuse, backup/recovery, and operator foundations.
- Artistic assets become first-class campaign creatives rather than a separate check-box feature.

## v2.1 — Domains and Scale

- Custom domains and managed TLS.
- Richer analytics and export.
- Artistic bulk generation with per-item scan proof.
- Teams, agency controls, APIs/webhooks, and higher-volume workflows.

## Sequencing rule

No post-MVP platform work starts merely because an agent is available. The Artistic MVP must first pass its sponsor-approved creative, scan-reliability, safety/privacy, performance, cost, and independent release gates.
